# Step 11: Launch On The App Store

## Goal

Ship Wikis publicly on the iOS App Store.

## Build

Prepare the app, content, legal, metadata, and monitoring for public release.

## Tasks

1. Finalize app name, subtitle, and description.
2. Prepare screenshots.
3. Prepare app preview video if useful.
4. Finalize privacy policy.
5. Finalize terms if accounts exist.
6. Complete App Store privacy nutrition labels.
7. Complete age rating.
8. Prepare review notes.
9. Submit build for review.
10. Respond to App Review if needed.
11. Release manually or with phased rollout.
12. Monitor crash reports and backend health.

## Launch Positioning

Position Wikis as:

```text
Human knowledge, one fascinating idea at a time.
```

Avoid positioning it as:

- AI Wikipedia
- a course app
- a social learning network
- a search replacement

## Acceptance Criteria

- App is approved by Apple.
- Production backend is stable.
- Support/contact path works.
- Bad content can be disabled quickly.
- App analytics are live.
- Crash monitoring is live.
- The first-session experience is excellent.

## Immediate Post-Launch Tasks

- Watch crashes.
- Watch topic load failures.
- Watch bad-card reports.
- Watch traversal dead ends.
- Add high-demand missing topics.
- Improve starter pool.
